[01]Project overview

4.8 stars. A profile score of 58/100.

An elite 4.8-star review base, held back by compliance and visibility gaps across maps and conversational AI search.

Shahi Manjil operates from a premium location at 3820 Victoria Park Ave in North York, Toronto, serving a dense culinary catchment area that spans North York, Scarborough, Markham, and the broader Greater Toronto Area (GTA). Spearheaded by four specialist regional chefs operating a strict policy of using no MSG or artificial colors, the restaurant delivers authentic Indian fine dining.

Despite boasting an elite 4.8-star review base, a comprehensive digital audit uncovered significant technical and operational visibility gaps. The profile suffered from a severe business name guideline violation (creating a high suspension risk), zero active Google Posts engagement, unpopulated Services and Q&A fields, and a total lack of structured booking deep links to convert high-intent maps traffic. This resulted in an overall GBP Score of just 58/100, causing a severe 'Visibility Gap' across traditional maps and conversational AI search engines.

[02]Our approach

Three phases.

We executed a targeted 30-day structural visibility framework designed to fix foundational operational elements, build real-time social proof, and optimize the brand for modern AI-driven Search/Generative Engine Optimization (SEO/GEO).

Phase 1 — Foundational Profile & Compliance Rebuild

  1. Guideline Compliance Cleanup: Trimmed the business name from its keyword-stuffed variation down to the compliant storefront name: 'Shahi Manjil' to eliminate profile suspension risks
  2. Conversion Streamlining: Integrated direct booking and order URLs inside the profile dashboard, linking OpenTable to eliminate conversion friction.
  3. Category Restructuring: Locked in 'Indian Restaurant' as the verified primary category and added four rich secondary categories (Fine Dining Restaurant, Catering Food and Drink Supplier, Event Venue, and South Asian Restaurant).
  4. Search Engine Optimization (SEO) Copywriting: Deployed a geo-tagged, paste-ready 750-character business description utilizing organic high-intent keywords within the first 100 characters

Phase 2 — Three-Vertical Service & Query Catalog

  1. 15-Entry Service Grid: Built and deployed 15 fully optimized, geo-targeted custom service items (e.g., Indian Catering Services Toronto, Halal Indian Restaurant, Biryani Restaurant Toronto) to populate Google's search justification snippets.
  2. Authority Seeded Q&A: Programmed and deployed 5 strategic community question-and-answer pairs addressing parking accessibility, vegetarian parameters, large group configurations, and signature dish entries.

Phase 3 — Social Proof & Authority Engine

  1. Structured Content Cadence: Implemented a consistent post schedule of 3 update/offer assets per week, indexing active search phrases like "Best Indian Restaurant Toronto".
  2. Review Infrastructure Calibration: Implemented a comprehensive review tracking and structured response strategy using specialized sentiment templates to maximize local engagement metrics.
  3. Advanced AI Schema Architecture: Injected custom LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema code directly into the brand's core domain framework, defining geographic variables, pricing indexes, and exact coordinates so AI models (ChatGPT, Gemini, and Apple Maps) can accurately cross-reference and recommend the business entity
